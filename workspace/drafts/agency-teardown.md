# Raw Notes: Why We Fired Our Marketing Agency

- Spent $14,500/month for 6 months.
- Deliverables: 4 blog posts a month that nobody read, and 8 generic LinkedIn drafts.
- The founder spent 4 hours every Sunday re-writing the drafts because they sounded like generic ChatGPT PR releases.
- Takeaway: Nobody outside your company can capture your nuanced product voice unless you feed them exact practitioner context.
