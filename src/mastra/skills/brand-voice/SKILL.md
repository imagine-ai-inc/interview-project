# Skill: Brand Voice & Tone Enforcement

Use this skill whenever drafting or revising LinkedIn posts, emails, or tweets for ImagineAI.

## Rules
- Tone must be bold, authoritative, practitioner-first.
- Strip all corporate buzzwords: "delve", "testament", "supercharge", "revolutionary".
- Use short sentences with strong linebreaks.
- Include a specific, low-friction call-to-action inviting conversation.
