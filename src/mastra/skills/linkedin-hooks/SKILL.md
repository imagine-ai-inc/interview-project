# Skill: B2B LinkedIn Hook Formulas

A LinkedIn post wins or loses in the first 2 lines before the "see more" fold.

## Top 3 Formulas
1. **The Contrarian Stat**: "90% of B2B marketing teams are moving fast in the wrong direction."
2. **The Direct Cost**: "We burned $15k/month on content agencies before we realized this one bottleneck."
3. **The Teardown Comparison**: "Founders think AI content is cheap. In reality, generic AI content costs you pipeline."
